## World 3: US East

- Working world name: `US East`
- Alternate name if we want a more family/travel feel: `Alabama & the Gulf`
- Reasoning: `US East` is broad enough to include Mobile, Atlanta, Tuscaloosa, New Orleans, and Pensacola without feeling geographically wrong. `Alabama` alone feels too narrow once New Orleans, Pensacola, and Atlanta enter the map.
- Villain candidate: `The Swamp Shadow`
- Villain concept origin: formerly sketched as `La Sombra del Pantano`; use the English name for this American/Southeastern world.
- Villain feel: a low, broad swamp entity made from cypress roots, muddy water, Spanish moss, old dock wood, reeds, and storm mist. It should have subtle alligator-inspired shapes near the waterline, but not be a literal alligator. It must look clearly different from El Cucuy del Desierto and stay scary but kid-appropriate.
- Crux Sacra color: emerald green with warm gold highlights
- Unlock pattern: the redeemed good character from World 2 becomes available in World 3.
- Intro video rule: show the chosen hero, chosen companion, Timmy, and The Swamp Shadow in the first US East setting.
- Ending video rule: same redemption structure as World 1 and World 2, but the final setting should match the World 3 boss location.

### Levels

1. Mobile, AL - Rainy Government Boulevard
   - Feel: rainy Mobile street inspired by Government Boulevard, broad multi-lane road, low Gulf Coast commercial buildings, wet asphalt, traffic lights, downtown/port hints in the distance, Spanish moss at the edges.
   - Gameplay: gentle opening level, wider safe lanes, Crux Sacras introduced in emerald green.
   - Visual note: should feel like a real Gulf Coast city/port, not a generic cartoon town.

2. Tuscaloosa, AL - University Stadium Campus
   - Feel: university campus walkway with brick buildings, stadium exterior in the distance, crimson-and-white game-day atmosphere, lights, banners, and campus energy.
   - Gameplay: moving crowd-shadow hazards, faster enemy bursts, Crux Sacras spread around gates and walkways.
   - Visual note: avoid official logo dependency; evoke the stadium/campus with crimson, white, lights, and football atmosphere.

3. Atlanta, GA - Centennial Olympic Park
   - Feel: Centennial Olympic Park-inspired urban plaza, fountains, ring-like water feature, skyline, wide park paths, evening city lights.
   - Gameplay: more vertical movement space, lightning more frequent, enemies appear from city shadows.
   - Visual note: should feel urban and polished, not dark-only.

4. Pensacola, FL - Sunny Gulf Beach
   - Feel: sunny beach boardwalk, turquoise water, white sand, dunes, pier, bright Gulf Coast sky.
   - Gameplay: wind gusts and fire hazards mixed with water-side routes; Holy Water remains useful but thunder cannot be blocked.
   - Visual note: colorful coastal level, a breather visually but harder mechanically.

5. New Orleans, LA - French Quarter Night
   - Feel: wrought-iron balconies, lanterns, wet streets, jazz-night color, old city mystery, “bad town” atmosphere implied by tavern lights and shadowy party silhouettes.
   - Gameplay: maze-like street flow, hazards come around corners, Rosary power especially valuable.
   - Visual note: spooky and elegant, with adult/bad-town ambience implied but still kid-safe and not horror-realistic.

6. Mobile Bay / Gulf Storm Boss
   - Feel: bay bridge / port lights / storm clouds / swamp edges, alligators partly visible in the background, final arena for The Swamp Shadow.
   - Gameplay: boss is larger and more aggressive, throws shadow-fire and storm bursts; more lightning strikes, harder Crux Sacra pressure.
   - Ending: Jesus heals The Swamp Shadow, who becomes the redeemed character assigned to the selected main hero for World 3.
