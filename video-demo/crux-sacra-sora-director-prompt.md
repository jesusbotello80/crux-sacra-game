# CRUX SACRA - Sora Director Prompt

Create a polished 45-second cinematic video game trailer for a bilingual family adventure game titled **CRUX SACRA**.

Style: premium animated feature / high-end video game trailer, clean expressive cartoon characters, rich painterly 3D-2D hybrid backgrounds, cinematic lighting, smooth character animation, emotionally warm but adventurous. Avoid flat kindergarten-style scenery, avoid static sticker cutouts, avoid characters sliding across the screen. Use real walk cycles, body acting, hand gestures, running, turning, reacting, and camera movement.

Setting: Colorado Springs, Colorado. Include a cozy family home with mountain views, a bright summer outdoor park, a snowy winter outdoor level, and a modern Catholic church interior with stained glass and warm sacred light.

Characters:
- Mr Chuy: father hero, warm and brave, must resemble the original Codex pet character face and proportions.
- Mrs Favi: mother hero, lady in her 40s, hiking/outdoor style, solid character, not transparent.
- Ñaña: young woman in early 20s, normal adult height and body style, not childlike.
- Abba: teenage girl, 15, slender but natural, not chubby.
- Ttitín: teenage boy, 17.
- Tata: teenage girl, 16, Ttitín's girlfriend.
- Elayitas: boy, 5, baseball outfit, full shoes visible, energetic.
- Nangie: toddler girl, 2, full shoes visible, curious and brave.
- Timmy: husky family pet, full body visible, heroic companion, scaled appropriately next to the kids.
- El Tacalache: villain, scary fantasy creature who hunts mischievous children, threatening but suitable for a family game trailer.
- Guardian Angel: luminous helper in the same clean cartoon style as the family.
- Saint Michael the Archangel: heroic helper, full body visible, armor, wings, sword and shield, same cartoon style as the rest.

Story premise:
A full family in Colorado Springs must save the kids and other mischievous children from El Tacalache. El Tacalache se come a los niños traviesos. The kids discover that the Saint Benedict Cross is their main power. Their light comes from the prayer **Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux**, and they pray the Our Father in Latin, English, and Spanish: Pater Noster, Our Father, Padre Nuestro. The Guardian Angel and Saint Michael the Archangel join them as helpers.

Important continuity and framing rules:
- All characters must be full-body when standing or running; no cut feet, no missing shoes, no cropped torsos.
- Character sizes must feel normalized: adults taller, teenagers medium, Elayitas child-sized, Nangie toddler-sized but not tiny, Timmy dog-sized and visible.
- Do not place large labels or captions over character bodies. Keep subtitles small, cinematic, and in the lower safe area.
- Keep all characters in a consistent cartoon/video-game style.
- Use cinematic camera moves: dolly-in, parallax, tracking shots, dramatic reveal, slow push toward the cross.
- Do not show watermarks, UI artifacts, frame borders, or random text.

Shot list:
1. 0-4s - Opening title. Sunrise over Pikes Peak and Colorado Springs. A glowing Saint Benedict Cross appears in the sky. Title: **CRUX SACRA**. Small subtitle: "A Colorado Springs Family Adventure / Una aventura familiar en Colorado Springs."
2. 4-8s - Family home level. The full family gathers outside a warm Colorado Springs home. Timmy runs to the kids. The camera pushes forward like a game level intro.
3. 8-13s - Summer park level. Elayitas runs in his baseball outfit, Nangie toddles after him, Timmy bounds beside them. Teenagers and parents move into formation. Real character animation, no sliding.
4. 13-17s - Winter snow level. Snowy Colorado Springs park. The kids follow glowing footprints in the snow. Timmy sniffs and alerts them. Family protects the children.
5. 17-22s - Villain reveal. Night falls. El Tacalache appears from red shadow among trees near the city lights. He reaches toward mischievous children, but the Saint Benedict Cross begins to glow.
6. 22-28s - Sacred power. Close-up of Elayitas and Nangie holding the glowing cross together. They sing softly: "Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux." The light expands in gold waves.
7. 28-33s - Modern Catholic church level. Stained glass, warm light, reverent atmosphere. The family prays together. Visual text appears briefly and tastefully around the cross, not over faces: "Pater Noster / Our Father / Padre Nuestro."
8. 33-38s - Helpers arrive. The Guardian Angel descends in warm white light. Saint Michael the Archangel lands heroically with shield and sword, full body visible. Both match the cartoon style.
9. 38-43s - Boss level montage. The family, Timmy, Guardian Angel, and Saint Michael face El Tacalache. The cross flashes with the Saint Benedict prayer initials: C.S.S.M.L., N.D.S.M.D., V.R.S., N.S.M.V., S.M.Q.L., I.V.B. The darkness retreats.
10. 43-45s - End card. The family stands safe at sunrise with Colorado Springs behind them. Title: **CRUX SACRA**. Subtitle: "Coming Soon / Próximamente."

Spanish narrator:

"El Tacalache se come a los niños traviesos."
"Juntos salvarán a los niños y a la ciudad."

Kids singing:
Elayitas and Nangie sing softly and reverently:
"Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux. Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux."

Music and sound:
Original cinematic orchestral score with warm strings, light choir, soft bells, adventure percussion, and sacred golden tones. Add childlike but reverent singing for the Crux Sacra phrase. Include subtle whooshes for the cross light, snow ambience, park ambience, church reverb, Timmy paw sounds, and a low ominous sound for El Tacalache.

Subtitle rules:
Use small bilingual subtitles only for narration and the key Latin phrase. Place subtitles in the lower safe area. Do not cover faces, feet, or bodies.

Final mood:
Family adventure, faith, courage, protection, Colorado Springs, sacred light defeating darkness. High-quality animated game trailer, suitable for a family audience.
