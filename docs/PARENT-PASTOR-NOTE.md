# Crux Sacra — note for a parent or a pastor

One page. Updated 2026-10-02. Live game: https://crux-sacra.fjfaithandfamily.com/game/

## What the child is playing

A free Catholic adventure in English and Spanish. The child picks a hero, walks a world, and collects glowing Crux Sacras. Villains are not killed. They are freed and can become companions. Prayer light, Holy Water, and the Rosary are the tools. There is a short tutorial the first time, on the Colorado world, and it can be skipped.

## What it is not

No ads. No chat. No account. No purchases inside the game. Progress stays on that phone or computer. Reset Progress in the game clears it. A prize, a medal, or a QR code must never unlock a world. El Rancho is not open yet.

## A parish night

Twenty minutes is enough. Use the church wifi or ask families to use their own phones. Start on Colorado. An adult can press T to skip the tutorial. The child should be able to move, pick up a cross, pray, and spray without someone reading the help page first. If they cannot, write down the exact screen. That note is more useful than a compliment.

Ask the pastor for one sentence: would you hand this link to a parent this week, and why or why not.

## What we are still checking

Labels such as locked, retry, and the tutorial are bilingual. We have not finished a line-by-line pass of every stage intro. Do not promise a Spanish-complete game until that pass is written down. Touch controls exist for phones. A live play on a real phone is still the test that counts.
