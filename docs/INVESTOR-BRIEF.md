## Why it can win

- Catholic kids' play in Spanish and English, built on real sacramentals (Crux Sacra, Holy Water, Rosary, prayer light), not a generic values skin.
- Safety is the product: no ads, no chat, no purchases. A physical prize must never sell an unlock.
- The build is real and versioned. A release gate runs before publish. Both hosts are checked for drift.

## What a check is for (in order)

Nothing later starts until the step before it is true.

1. This page stays matched to live. No billion-Catholic slide in the room.
2. Spanish on the lines a child reads, touch play on a phone, and a one-page note for a parent and a pastor. El Rancho stays locked until that note exists.
3. A privacy-safe count of sessions and return visits. Success is a number, not a dashboard project.
4. Five family playtests and one parish night you can drive to. Write what the child did without help, plus one sentence from the pastor.
5. Then one small sale: a few dozen medal-and-card kits, or one school pack. A QR may preview a companion. It must not grant progress.
6. Native app, soundtrack, El Rancho, and the sibling titles wait until kids finish a world and a parent asks for the class.

## The ask

Fund steps 2 through 4 as one milestone. Look at a merch test only after the parish night. Do not fund a 25,000-unit medal run, a native port, or an orchestral score on this page.
