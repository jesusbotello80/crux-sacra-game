# Crux Sacra 1 — Investor Brief (one page)

**Product:** Bilingual (EN/ES) Catholic family action game. Kids play heroes rescuing glowing Crux Sacras from villains across 10 worlds, redeeming companions, unlocking Holy Land + Saints bonus. Live in production (v110).
**Play:** https://crux-sacra.fjfaithandfamily.com/game/ (Cloudflare Pages mirror included)
**Deep plan:** `docs/BUSINESS-AND-MERCHANDISE-PLAN.md` (market sizing, merch lines, parish kits, projections, seed use-of-funds).

## Why it can win

- **Underserved niche:** faithful Catholic kids' gaming in Spanish + English, built with real sacramentals (Crux Sacra, Holy Water, Rosary, prayer light) — not generic "values" skin.
- **Safety as the moat:** no ads, no chat, no purchases, progress stays on-device. A parent note + full guide ship in-game. Trust is the product for this buyer (parents, parishes, schools).
- **Real production discipline:** versioned releases (v109), release gate on every push, dual-host deploy with drift checks, bilingual docs, AI-assisted team (Codex, OpenCode, Muse Code) under one owner.

## Honest status (no vanity metrics)

- **Players/revenue:** not yet instrumented — no dashboard to show. Privacy-friendly edge analytics exist on the canonical host (Cloudflare beacon); product metrics are a queued packet, trackers stay banned by design.
- **Sales/merch:** nothing built. No store, no donation path, no parish/school licensing. Distribution is one URL today.
- **Team risk:** single owner + AI lanes. Bus factor is the top risk; mitigated by docs, gates, and reproducible deploys, not by headcount.
- **Known gaps (from 2026-09-28 persona roundtable, 35 findings):** Spanish gameplay text incomplete (~50 level intros EN-only), touch-control accessibility P0, no mid-world checkpoints. Fixed in v110: guide name drift (Nangie/Don Maro/chains), travel-banner lock bug, walk-cycle sprites. Ranked backlog + packets in `docs/reviews/M-A-PERSONA-ROUNDTABLE-2026-09-28.md`.

## What money buys (milestone tranches)

1. **Trust + reach closed-loop:** bilingual gameplay text, a11y pass, privacy-safe metrics dashboard, parent/parish one-pager. Success = measurable play sessions + returning families.
2. **Revenue path (Owner picks one first):** parish/school licensing, merch (plush/print of redeemed companions), or donations/sponsorship. Success = first dollar + repeatable channel.
3. **Content scale:** El Rancho public launch, Saints expansion, second title cross-promotion (maze, catechism quest, baseball exist as siblings).

## The ask

Milestone-based support, tranche by tranche, each gated on shipped packets above — not a blind check. First demo of tranche 1 is weeks of lane work, already packeted on `docs/AI-DISPATCH.md`.
